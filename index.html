Never destined for an ordinary life.

From the streets of Brooklyn to the nightlife of Atlanta, Dorian Bennett, known to the world as Freaky Focus, was never destined to live an ordinary life.

Growing up surrounded by ambition, loyalty, street politics, and the relentless pursuit of respect, Focus learns early that survival requires more than strength. It demands intelligence, instincts, and the ability to recognize danger before it arrives.

Music opens doors to a bigger world, but success brings new complications. As Focus navigates the rap industry, friendships, family, and relationships, the stakes rise far beyond record deals and recognition.

What begins as a journey through hip-hop culture, brotherhood, and the streets evolves into a high-stakes action thriller involving dangerous alliances, international intrigue, and powerful people operating behind the scenes.

From Brooklyn to Atlanta and beyond, Focus discovers that the greatest threats aren't always the ones standing directly in front of him. Some people are built to follow the rules. Others were born to rewrite them.

The rapper is only one side of the story

Before the classified missions. Before the covert operations. Before he becomes a man the world will never truly know, there is Freaky Focus: a rising hip-hop star chasing money, women, fame, and a life beyond anything he ever imagined.

The prologue of I'm Not Just Anybody gives a glimpse of a dangerous future, where Focus's world extends far beyond music and powerful people are paying attention. What happens in between is where the series lives.

Meet the author

Cinematic stories of power and love

Avery McIntyre is a multi-genre fiction author creating cinematic stories of ambition, power, love, loyalty, betrayal, and survival.

His work spans action thrillers, contemporary Black romance, supernatural crime fiction, and romantic espionage. McIntyre is the creator of the Freaky Focus Universe, beginning with I'm Not Just Anybody, a hip-hop action thriller about an ambitious artist drawn into a world far more dangerous than music.
